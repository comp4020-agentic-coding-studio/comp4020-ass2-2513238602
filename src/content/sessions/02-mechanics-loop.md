Start in Practice Tool, remove unnecessary variables, then add movement, target switching, and time pressure. Volibear students test Q approach routes, E placement timing, W re-cast windows, and safe exit paths.

Finish with a short transfer block against a partner. If the mechanic disappears under opposition, lower the difficulty and rebuild it.
