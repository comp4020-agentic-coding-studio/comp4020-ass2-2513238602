## The brief

> Defend the current version of your champion mastery model, then show how you will keep it trustworthy when the game changes.

Re-test your ten hardest interactions over three cycles. Integrate mechanics, lane plans, setup choices, jungle pressure, side-lane decisions, objectives, and teamfight jobs into one navigable playbook. Every “mastered” claim must include its evidence and the condition that triggers a future re-test.

## What you submit

- a living champion playbook with full-roster interaction coverage;
- the ten-counter, three-cycle evidence matrix;
- an eight-minute live or recorded demonstration;
- a two-minute response to an unseen transfer scenario;
- a 600-word maintenance plan for future patches.

The capstone rewards a model that can be challenged and improved, not one that pretends to be final.
