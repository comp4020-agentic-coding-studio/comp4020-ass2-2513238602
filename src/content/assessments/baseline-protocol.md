## The brief

> Prove, revise, or reject one matchup rule using a test another student could reproduce.

Choose an interaction that currently changes how you play lane. State the prediction before testing and include the opponent's likely counter-response. Run at least three resets, swap sides or pilots where possible, and separate execution failures from a weak plan.

## What you submit

- a two-page protocol in PDF or accessible web format;
- a three- to five-minute annotated replay excerpt;
- the before-and-after atlas entry;
- a 150-word next-test note.

Strong work does not need a winning result. It needs a transparent method and a conclusion proportionate to the evidence.
