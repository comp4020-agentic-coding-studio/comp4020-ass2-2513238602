## Re-test, do not rehearse success

Students nominate ten high-risk matchups from their own evidence. Each receives three test cycles with a changed condition: side selection, jungle pressure, item timing, wave state, or opponent plan.

The goal is not to force a positive result. It is to produce a rule that survives variation and clearly identifies when it should not be used.

Peers challenge unsupported claims, missing counterplay, and plans that only work when already ahead.
