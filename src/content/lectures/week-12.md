## Mastery is maintained

A patch can alter numbers, items, objectives, or common opponents. Identify which assumptions changed, run the smallest useful validation test, and preserve unaffected knowledge.

The final playbook contains confident rules, conditional rules, unresolved questions, and a ranked next-test queue. Honest uncertainty is more useful than false completeness.

Plan, drill, test, review, and revise. Graduation means owning that loop without needing a tutor to initiate it.
