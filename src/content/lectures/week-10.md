## Coverage at two resolutions

Common lane opponents receive direct one-versus-one tests. Every other champion still receives an interaction entry: what they can interrupt, kite, lock down, deny, or enable when encountered elsewhere on the map.

This is how “train against every champion” remains rigorous: depth for common lanes, complete recognition coverage for the roster, and escalation to direct testing whenever uncertainty or importance is high.

No atlas entry can be marked mastered without evidence and a scheduled re-test.
