## Lane is a system

Trading cannot be separated from the minion wave. We ask who reaches the next level first, whose wave deals more damage, where the next wave will meet, and what each player can buy after the exchange.

Students annotate the first four waves of two contrasting lanes and identify the moment when the original plan should change.

## Transfer

Every matchup page must include an opening-wave intention and at least one condition that invalidates it.
