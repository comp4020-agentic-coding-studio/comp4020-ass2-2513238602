## Role before action

The same champion can begin a fight, protect a carry, split the enemy formation, or hold a choke. Identify the job from both compositions, cooldowns, terrain, and objective stakes.

Students review one fight three times: at thirty seconds before contact, at first commitment, and at the point of no return.

The Volibear case compares tower pressure, flank access, front-line disruption, and peel. The chosen job must be stated before evaluating execution.
