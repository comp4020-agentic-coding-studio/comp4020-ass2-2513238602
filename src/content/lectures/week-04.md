## Counter is conditional

“Counter” is not a complete explanation. A difficult lane might deny the first wave, interrupt an engage, out-sustain short trades, or punish a specific cooldown. Each claim needs a time, position, resource state, and response.

We group opponents by interaction pattern, then test individuals. This reduces preparation time without pretending two champions in the same class behave identically.

## Volibear lens

Compare a ranged-spacing test, a disengage test, and an extended-fight test, then decide what evidence would overturn each prediction.
