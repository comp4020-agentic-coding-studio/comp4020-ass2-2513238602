## The lane is not one-versus-one

We infer likely jungle windows from leash information, camp timing, lane priority, and objective state. The response may be a ward, a smaller trade, a delayed crash, a cross-map call, or accepting a controlled loss.

Students rehearse three versions of the same matchup: isolated lane, allied jungle pressure, and enemy jungle pressure.

Weak-side success is measured by resources preserved, information created, and pressure absorbed—not only by solo kills.
