## The transition problem

Winning lane does not automatically create a winning map. We track the moment lane assignments break, then compare side-wave value, objective setup, teleport availability, and who can safely collect the next resource.

Students build a three-minute decision tree for their champion: ahead, even, and behind.

The portfolio review checks whether matchup learning changes global choices rather than remaining a collection of isolated lane notes.
