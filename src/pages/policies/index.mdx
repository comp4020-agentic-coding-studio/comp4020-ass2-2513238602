# Policies and support

## Practice conduct

Partner drills are cooperative experiments. Follow the agreed setup, disclose
when you change a condition, rotate roles, and critique the decision or
evidence—not the player. Harassment, griefing, account sharing, boosting, or
deliberate disruption is incompatible with the course.

## Evidence and privacy

Use recordings you are entitled to share. Remove player names from public
submissions unless everyone has consented. Never submit login credentials,
private chat logs, or another person's replay as your own evidence. Cite
external statistics, guides, coaching, and AI assistance where they materially
shaped a decision.

## Late work and extensions

Contact the convenor before the deadline when possible. Approved extensions
replace the published deadline. Without an extension, work loses 5% of the
available mark per calendar day and is not accepted after seven days, because
later reviews depend on the submitted artefact.

## Accessibility and safer participation

Equivalent evidence formats are available for students who cannot record
voice, play long blocks, or participate in live one-versus-one practice.
Contact the convenor to arrange shorter sets, asynchronous review, captions,
reduced sensory load, or another reasonable adjustment without lowering the
learning standard.

## Patch and platform disclaimer

Patch-specific claims must record the tested version and be revalidated after
relevant changes. This fictional educational course is not endorsed by Riot
Games. League of Legends and Riot Games are trademarks of their respective
owner; no Riot artwork or game assets are required for submission.

## Getting help

Post shared questions on the forum, bring timestamped clips to Champion Labs,
or book a clinic with the teaching team. For urgent wellbeing, safety, or
accessibility matters, use the university's formal student-support channels.
